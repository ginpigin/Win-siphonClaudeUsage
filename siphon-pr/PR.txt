TITLE:
feat(ui): show weekly quota in the mini floating widget

DESCRIPTION:
## What

The mini (pill) floating widget now shows the **weekly (7-day) quota** next to the session percent.

- A hairline divider and the existing `menu-weekly` calendar icon separate the two values.
- The weekly number is tinted by its own level (`warn` / `high` / `critical`), so a filling week stands out even while the session icon is still green.
- Respects the `used` / `remaining` display mode, like the rest of the widget.

## Details

- `floating.html` / `floating.js`: new weekly group in `.floating-mini-row`, rendered from `state.quota.weeklyAll`.
- `styles.css`: mini row content is centered; new `.floating-mini-weekly-*` styles.
- `floating.rs`: `MINI` widened from 71 to 136 px to fit the worst case (`100% | 100%`). On restore, a saved position is pulled back inside its monitor so a pill parked against the right edge does not hang off it after the resize.

## Testing

- `npm test` (142 pass) and `npm run lint` are clean.
- Built for `x86_64-pc-windows-msvc` and running on Windows 11.
